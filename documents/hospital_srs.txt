Project: Hospital Management System

Purpose:
The system manages hospital operations including patient registration, appointments, doctors, billing, and medical records.

Functional Requirements:

* Patient registration and login
* Doctor management
* Appointment scheduling
* Electronic medical records
* Billing and payment processing
* Laboratory report management
* Prescription management
* Admin dashboard

Non-Functional Requirements:

* Secure patient data storage
* Role-based access control
* High availability
* Fast response time
* Data backup and recovery

User Stories:

* As a patient, I can book appointments online.
* As a doctor, I can view patient history.
* As an admin, I can manage doctors and staff.

Scope:
The system digitizes hospital operations and improves patient management.
