Project: E-Commerce Platform

Purpose:
The system enables online buying and selling of products.

Functional Requirements:

* User registration and login
* Product catalog management
* Product search and filtering
* Shopping cart
* Order placement
* Online payment
* Order tracking
* Review and ratings

Non-Functional Requirements:

* Secure payment gateway integration
* Fast search response
* Scalability during peak traffic
* Mobile responsiveness

User Stories:

* As a customer, I can purchase products online.
* As a seller, I can manage inventory.
* As an admin, I can manage users and products.

Scope:
Provide a complete online shopping experience.
