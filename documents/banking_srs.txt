Project: Banking Management System

Purpose:
The system provides online banking services for customers and administrators.

Functional Requirements:

* Customer registration and login
* Account management
* Balance inquiry
* Fund transfer
* Transaction history
* Loan management
* Beneficiary management
* Account statement generation

Non-Functional Requirements:

* End-to-end encryption
* Multi-factor authentication
* Scalability
* 99.9% availability

User Stories:

* As a customer, I can transfer funds securely.
* As a customer, I can view transaction history.
* As an admin, I can manage customer accounts.

Scope:
Provide secure digital banking services.
