Project: Food Delivery System

Purpose:
The system connects customers, restaurants, and delivery partners.

Functional Requirements:

* Customer registration and login
* Restaurant listing
* Menu management
* Food search
* Order placement
* Online payment
* Real-time order tracking
* Delivery partner management

Non-Functional Requirements:

* Real-time updates
* High availability
* Secure payments
* Fast response time

User Stories:

* As a customer, I can order food online.
* As a restaurant owner, I can update menus.
* As a delivery partner, I can manage deliveries.

Scope:
Provide efficient food ordering and delivery services.
